# Zenysis

> Zenysis is a health intelligence infrastructure company that helps governments and organizations in low- and middle-income countries turn fragmented health data into actionable insight. Its flagship, open-source Harmony Analytics Platform transforms, cleans, and enriches data from multiple sources for analysis and decision-making. There is no public hosted API; Harmony is self-hosted and exposes its own REST (Flask-Potion) and GraphQL interfaces per deployment.
